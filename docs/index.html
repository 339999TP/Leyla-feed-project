What do the ratings mean?

Maturity stage

  1. Discovery — a brand-new finding or observation
  2. Lab — demonstrated under controlled conditions
  3. Prototype — early real-world device or test
  4. Commercial — in production / deployed
  5. Scaled — widespread, mature technology

Significance (1–5)

  • 1 Incremental — small step
  • 2 Notable — worth knowing
  • 3 Significant — solid advance
  • 4 Major breakthrough — field-shifting
  • 5 Landmark — historic result
